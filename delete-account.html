Delete Your Account

Account & Data Deletion · SolenLabs LLC

We're sorry to see you go. You can delete your Bonva account and all associated data using one of the methods below. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

⚠️ This action is permanent. Deleting your account will remove your profile, quiz history, relationship scores, partner linking, and all personal data from our servers. This cannot be reversed.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — name, partner name, relationship info
  • Your Relationship Quiz scores and history
  • Your streak, mood logs, bucket list, timeline, and special dates
  • Your partner room code and any partner linking
  • Your Supabase account and authentication credentials
  • Your daily question answers
  • Any clinical notes in the Therapist Review area

Note: Payment records are retained by Stripe as required by financial regulations. Nova conversation content is not stored on our servers.

1

Delete in the app — instant

The fastest way to delete your local data:

  1. Open Bonva at bonva.app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Reset & re-test onboarding
  4. Confirm — all local data is deleted immediately

This clears all data from your device. To also delete your Supabase account and server-side data, use the email request below.

2

Request full account deletion — via email

To permanently delete your Supabase account and all server-side data, submit a request below. We'll process it within 7 business days and send a confirmation email.
