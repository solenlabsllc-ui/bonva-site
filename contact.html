Common Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
Email support@bonva.app with your request and we'll cancel immediately. If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, you can also cancel directly in your device settings.
Can I get a refund?
Yes. We offer full refunds within 24 hours of any purchase. Email support@bonva.app and we'll process it right away.
I lost my data — can you restore it?
If you have an account, your profile data is stored in Supabase and can be restored by signing back in. Local data (quiz scores, bucket list) may not be recoverable if you cleared your browser. Email us anyway — we'll do our best to help.
Does Bonva work on desktop?
Yes! Bonva is fully available on the web at bonva.app — no app download required. Use it on any browser, desktop or mobile.
Is my data private?
We take privacy seriously. Most of your relationship data stays on your device. The only data we send externally is to our AI coaching feature, which uses Anthropic's Claude, and to Supabase for account and partner sync. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.
How does partner linking work?
One partner generates a room code in Settings → Partner. The other enters that code to link accounts. Once linked, you can compare quiz scores and see each other's daily question answers. Either person can unlink at any time.
